## Critical Path Items

These are the highest-leverage things the group can do right now, based on what's blocking the most downstream work:

### 🔴 LAPPD Raw Data Cleaning (Thread 3)
Most other threads are gated on having clean, trustworthy LAPPD data. Characterizing and correcting pedestal miscalibrations, PSEC wraparound artifacts, and RF noise is the single highest-leverage near-term activity.

### 🔴 Light Data Format & DQ (Thread 5)
Physics analyses (CC0pi, beam-timing, SBI) all need a processed, analysis-ready dataset. Getting the full ANNIE run into a lightweight format with known DQ flags unblocks everything downstream.

### 🟡 LitePath Validation (Thread 1)
The tank-based reconstruction and SBI framework both depend on a validated forward simulator. Completing the tuning and validation against data control samples (through-going muons) is the next major milestone.
