## Scientific Goals

ANNIE has two primary physics goals:

1. **Neutron multiplicity measurement** — measure the number of neutrons produced in neutrino-nucleus interactions as a function of neutrino energy, interaction channel, and nuclear target. This directly constrains neutrino interaction models used in oscillation experiments.

2. **LAPPD technology demonstration** — demonstrate precision photon detection timing in a real neutrino detector environment, paving the way for next-generation water Cherenkov experiments.

## Why Neutron Multiplicity Matters

Neutron production in neutrino interactions is poorly modeled and is a significant source of systematic uncertainty for experiments like T2K, NOvA, and future DUNE/HK. ANNIE provides the first direct measurement in a water target on the BNB.
