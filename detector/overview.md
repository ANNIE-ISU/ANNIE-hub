## What ANNIE Is

ANNIE (Accelerator Neutrino Neutron Interaction Experiment) is a water Cherenkov neutrino detector located at Fermilab, on the Booster Neutrino Beamline (BNB). Its primary scientific goal is to measure neutron multiplicity in neutrino-nucleus interactions, which is important for constraining neutrino interaction cross-sections and reducing systematic uncertainties in long-baseline oscillation experiments.

ANNIE is also a technology testbed, particularly for **Large Area Picosecond Photodetectors (LAPPDs)** — fast, large-format photodetectors that enable precision timing in water Cherenkov detectors.

## Location

Fermilab Booster Neutrino Beamline (BNB), Building 109 (SciBooNE hall).

## Why It Matters

Neutron tagging in water Cherenkov detectors is a key capability for next-generation experiments (HK, DUNE). ANNIE demonstrates this at scale and provides real neutrino interaction data for model validation.
